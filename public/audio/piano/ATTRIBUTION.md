# Piano sample attribution

These piano samples are adapted from the `piano` instrument in Nicholaus P. Brosowsky's
[tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments) library.

- Sample source identified by the library: VSO2 (Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2)
- Samples in tonejs-instruments: CC BY 3.0
- tonejs-instruments loader code: MIT, Copyright 2018 Nicholaus P. Brosowsky

Only a reduced set of pitches is shipped here. Tone.js repitches the closest samples to cover
the piano range used by Key Changer.
